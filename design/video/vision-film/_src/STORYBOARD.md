## Plans réalistes à générer (prompts)
Même personnage dans les 4 plans : homme d'environ 25 ans, cheveux bruns bouclés courts, barbe légère, sweat gris. Étalonnage sarcelle / vert sombre, grain 35 mm, pas de texte lisible à l'écran.

1. Night, small bedroom lit only by a phone screen. The man lies in bed scrolling his phone with his thumb, face lit cold blue by the screen, slow push-in.
2. Night. He sits up, walks to a desk and opens a laptop in the dark; the screen light rises on his face. Handheld, slow.
3. Close-up. Laptop light on his face, he rubs his eyes with both hands, tired and disappointed. Static, shallow depth of field.
4. Morning daylight, same desk. He reads something on his phone, a small relieved smile, coffee mug on the desk. Slow push-in.
