What's broken with CRO today:

1. Too expensive. A CRO agency or team costs more than most stores can spend. So most small stores never run a single real test.
2. Too slow. Audit, meetings, mockups, development. Weeks before the first test. Meanwhile, the leaks keep leaking.
3. Opinions, not tests. Many recommendations are based on taste, not on real sales. If it isn't tested, it's still a guess.
4. No memory. When the agency leaves, what it learned leaves with it. Every test should make the next one smarter.
