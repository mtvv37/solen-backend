Your return policy is a sales tool:

1. Say it next to the button. The fear of being stuck with the wrong product stops the click. One short line under “Add to cart”: how many days, how easy.
2. Make it simple to read. A legal wall of text sounds like you're hiding something. 3 plain sentences: how long, who pays, how to start a return.
3. Answer “who pays?. Unclear return shipping costs create doubt. Say it clearly, even if the customer pays.
4. Show it on mobile too. On a phone, the footer is a long scroll away. Put it in the product page itself, not only in a separate page.
