Your product description is costing you sales:

1. Features instead of benefits. “100% linen” is a fact. “Stays cool on hot days” is a reason to buy. Lead with what the product does for them.
2. A wall of text. Nobody reads a long paragraph on a phone. 3 short bullets first, the full story below.
3. Only studio photos. White background shows what it is, not what it's like to own it. Add 1 or 2 photos of the product in use, early in the gallery.
4. Missing details. Size, material, care, what's in the box. Every gap is a reason to leave. Answer the questions your customers ask by email.
5. Generic supplier copy. The same text as 50 other stores gives no reason to buy from you. Rewrite it in your own words, for your own customers.
