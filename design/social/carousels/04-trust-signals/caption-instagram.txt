5 trust signals every product page needs:

1. Reviews near the price. Proof that other people bought and liked it, right where the decision happens. Star rating and review count under the title, photos from customers below.
2. A clear return policy. “What if it doesn't fit?” is the question that stops the click. One line next to the button: how many days, who pays, how simple.
3. A delivery estimate. “When will I get it?” If you don't answer, they assume the worst. Show an estimated delivery date, not just “ships in X days”.
4. Recognizable payment methods. Logos they use every day make a new store feel safe. Show the payment options they know, near the buy button.
5. A real way to reach you. A store with no visible contact looks like it could disappear. Email, chat or a contact page linked from the product page.
