5 CRO myths that waste your time:

1. A red button converts better. No color wins everywhere. Contrast with your page matters, not the color itself. Test visibility, not a color you read about.
2. More traffic fixes everything. Sending more people to a page that leaks just leaks more. Fix the biggest leak first, then pay for traffic.
3. Copy what big brands do. Their audience, prices and trust level are not yours. Use them for ideas, then test on your own store.
4. A redesign will boost sales. A full redesign changes everything at once. If sales drop, you won't know why. Change one thing at a time and measure each one.
5. Best practices always work. A best practice is a good first guess, not a result. Treat every best practice as a test to run.
