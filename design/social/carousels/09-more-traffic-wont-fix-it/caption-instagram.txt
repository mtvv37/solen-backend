More traffic won't fix a leaking page:

1. The leaky bucket. Ads pour visitors in. Every leak on your page lets some of them out. Pour more and you mostly pay for more leaks.
2. Find where they leave. Product page, cart or checkout: one step usually loses more than the others. Compare visits, add to carts and orders in your Shopify analytics.
3. Fix the biggest hole. Start where the most people drop off. Test one fix on that step against your current page.
4. Then scale. A page that converts better makes every ad cheaper. Now more traffic actually means more sales.
