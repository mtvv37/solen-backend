5 leaks killing your Shopify sales:

1. Sizes hidden in a dropdown. Shoppers can't see what's in stock without clicking. Show sizes as buttons and cross out the sold-out ones.
2. Surprise shipping costs. An unexpected cost at checkout makes people leave. Show shipping and delivery date right under the price.
3. Reviews buried at the bottom. Most visitors never scroll that far. Put the star rating right under the product title.
4. Buy button hidden on mobile. If the button isn't on screen, the decision gets postponed. Move it up or make it sticky.
5. No answer to "will it fit me?". Doubt kills more sales than price. Add a size guide and one line on fit next to the sizes.
