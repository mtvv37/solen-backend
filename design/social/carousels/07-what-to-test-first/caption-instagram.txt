How to pick what to test first:

1. List your ideas. Write down every change you'd like to make, big or small. One line each: what you would change, and where.
2. Score the impact. How much could this move sales if it works? Pages with the most visitors and the clearest leak score highest.
3. Score your confidence. Do you have a reason to believe it, or is it a hunch? Customer messages, reviews or recordings count as evidence.
4. Score the effort. How long does it take to build and launch? Start with high impact, high confidence, low effort. Then repeat.
