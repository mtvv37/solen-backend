Why shoppers add to cart and never buy:

1. They were just saving it. Many people use the cart as a wishlist to compare later. Send a reminder email with the product photo and one reason to buy now.
2. The total surprised them. Shipping or fees show up and the price feels different. Show the full cost, shipping included, before the cart.
3. Checkout felt like work. Long forms and forced accounts make people quit halfway. Guest checkout and express payment buttons first.
4. They didn't trust the store yet. Paying a store you just discovered is a risk. Reviews, return policy and contact info visible in the cart.
5. They went to compare prices. Without a reason to choose you, they buy where it's cheaper or faster. Show what makes you different: delivery speed, returns, guarantee.
