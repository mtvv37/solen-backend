# Solen social content rules

Apply to every Instagram, TikTok and X post. No exceptions.

1. **No em dash (—) and no en dash (–), anywhere**: slides, captions, hashtags, alt text. Use a comma, a colon, a period or parentheses instead.
2. **Carousels: 7 slides max** (6 is fine). Hook, value slides, CTA.
3. **English only.**
4. **Solen visual identity**: dark fir background (#040A06), tilted rotating frame logo, Inter Tight / Archivo Expanded / JetBrains Mono, green accents (#2BD07A, #7EF0A5).
5. **CTA on the last slide**: "Comment AUDIT and I'll send you a free audit of your product page."
6. **No invented numbers**: no revenue claims, no "+X% conversion", no fake reviews. Present fixes as things to test, not guaranteed results.
7. **Two formats per carousel**: `ig/` 1080x1350 (4:5) and `tt/` 1080x1920 (9:16). On TikTok, keep text out of the bottom 420 px and the right 130 px (covered by the app UI).
8. **Captions: short and structured.** One hook line, then each tip as a numbered point with one or two short sentences of detail (the problem, then what to test), then the call to action: "Comment AUDIT for a free audit of your product page."
9. **No emoji** in captions or slides.
10. **Hashtags: exactly `#shopify #ecommerce`** on every post. Nothing else.
