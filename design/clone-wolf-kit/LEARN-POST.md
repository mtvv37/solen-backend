# Learn post type (Solen)

One short that teaches one checklist in about 30 to 40 seconds. Educational and honest: things to check and test, never guaranteed results.

## Structure

| Time | Beat | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | Hook | Promise the checklist, clear payoff | Pill "LEARN", big headline, one gradient word |
| 3 to 6 s | Open loop | Tease the one most people skip | Punch caption only |
| 6 to 30 s | 3 steps, about 8 s each | The thing, then why it matters, one or two short sentences | Pill "1 OF 3", mock UI, ring on the element, punch caption |
| 30 to 36 s | Payoff + ask | One line to act on, then the CTA | Logo, question, keyword box |

## Rules

- 3 steps, never more. One idea per frame, centered.
- Show what you name: the ring goes on the element the line talks about.
- No invented numbers, no revenue claims. Money written as words in the script.
- Something new on screen by about 2 seconds. Animate the verb.
- No em dash, no en dash, no emoji, English only.
- Hashtags: exactly `#shopify #ecommerce`.
- CTA Instagram: "Comment AUDIT for a free audit of your product page." TikTok: "Free audit of your product page. Link in bio."
- Reserve the CTA keyword with `keywords.py` before the first post.

## Hook formulas

- Before you [do the expensive thing], check [3 things].
- [Number] things your [page] needs before [goal].
- Most [people] skip number [one].
- Stop [common habit]. Check this instead.

## Voice-over rules

- Numbers as words, "A B test" instead of "A/B", no symbols.
- Short sentences, one idea each. A full stop is a real pause.
- About 150 words per minute. 85 words is about 34 seconds.
- Delivery: confident, warm, a little faster on the hook, slower on the open loop and on the final ask.
- Lock the voice-over text before the avatar render.
